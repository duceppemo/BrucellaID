Subject: SRR35797377 (PRJNA1290416) - your species is right; the library looks mixed

Dear colleagues,

We have been validating a Brucella species-identification tool against every public
Brucella read set in ENA. This is not a correction request: your deposited species
looks correct. I am writing because the record behaves oddly in automated screens and
you may want to know why.

    run          BioSample       deposited             what we find
    SRR35797377  SAMN49920683    Brucella melitensis   B. melitensis, plus other
                                                       organisms

Profiling the reads against GTDB r220 finds Brucella melitensis at 99.91% average
nucleotide identity with 97% of the reference genome present, at 7.2x coverage. That
is a genuine and nearly complete Brucella melitensis genome, so the deposited species
is supported.

Alongside it the reads carry other organisms at under 1x - a Xanthomonas at 0.64x and
a Cutibacterium at 0.14x - and the library assembles to 5.6 Mb in 10,733 contigs,
which is roughly 1.7 times the size of a Brucella genome and far more fragmented than
a clean 7x library would give.

The practical consequence is that sketch-based identification tools, including ours,
report this record as not identified. They measure what dominates a library rather
than what is present in it, and at 7x inside a mixed library the Brucella contributes
only about 26 of 1000 sketch hashes. Containment-based methods see it clearly;
abundance-based ones do not. That is a limitation of the tools rather than a fault in
your record, but it does mean the run will keep failing automated Brucella screens.

If the isolate is still held and a cleaner library is ever convenient, it would be a
useful record to have at depth. Nothing needs changing otherwise.
