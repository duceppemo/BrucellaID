Subject: Third-party curation request: 13 Brucella BioSamples whose organism field is
         contradicted by the deposited reads, plus 11 flagged for annotation

To the ENA curation team,

We have validated a Brucella species-identification tool against every public Brucella
read set in ENA - 4,042 runs under NCBI taxon 234 - and are reporting the records where
the deposited organism does not match the sequence. We are not the submitter of any of
them, so this goes to you rather than to the records directly; we have also written to
the submitting groups and will copy you on anything they confirm or dispute.

Two requests, in decreasing order of confidence. Section 1 asks for a change. Section 2
asks only for an annotation.


1. THIRTEEN BIOSAMPLES: ORGANISM FIELD CONTRADICTED BY THE SEQUENCE

Each of these rests on two methods that share no code and no reference data: Mash
distance over a curated 431-genome reference set covering all 30 named species of the
clade, and the nine-locus Whatmore MLST scheme called from the reads. Where the species
is one the panel covers, the real-time assays of Hoover et al. 2026
(doi:10.1128/spectrum.02974-25) run in silico agree as a third line.

The Mash column is the distance to the nearest genome of the proposed species. For
context, two genomes of one Brucella species are typically 0.0001 to 0.002 apart, so
most of these are as close to the proposed species as members of one species get. The
ST column is the sequence type; PubMLST records ST1 as B. abortus in 99.4% of 466
isolates, ST5 as B. abortus in 98.6% of 71, ST14 as B. suis in 96.4% of 140, ST15 as
B. suis in 96.1% of 103, ST19 as B. suis in all 6, ST22 as B. neotomae in all 9, and
ST25 as B. pinnipedialis in 95.0% of 80. ERR2993145 has no ST called, but two
independent reference sets reach B. suis for it.

The libraries are sound: these assemble to 3.24-3.35 Mb, the normal size for a Brucella
genome, so they are good genomes carrying a different species name rather than poor
data misleading a distance.

One record from the same submission is deliberately NOT in the table. ERR2938687
(SAMEA5145007, deposited B. melitensis) has the largest distance of its group at
0.00591, by far the worst assembly at 2,165 contigs, and carries a complete B. suis
amplicon in 54% of informative reads alongside a B. melitensis amplicon in 33%. A mixed
library explains all three observations where a wrong species name explains none, so we
propose no new name and suggest only that it be flagged as a possible mixture.


2. ELEVEN BIOSAMPLES: DEPOSITED AS BRUCELLA, LIBRARY CONTAINS LITTLE OR NONE

No change of organism is requested here, because we cannot say what the submitter
intended. An annotation that the library does not contain the deposited organism would
stop these being downloaded as Brucella, which is how we came to them.

    ERR471337 (SAMEA2266897), ERR473743 (SAMEA2266955), ERR554820 (SAMEA2382534),
    ERR775236 (SAMEA2821524), ERR775237 (SAMEA2821525), ERR775244 (SAMEA2821533),
    ERR775245 (SAMEA2821534), ERR775246 (SAMEA2821535), ERR775247 (SAMEA2821536),
    ERR775248 (SAMEA2821537)  - all deposited as B. abortus
    ERR3218177 (SAMEA104338291) - deposited as B. abortus

These share 1 to 6 of 1000 k-mer hashes with any genome of the clade, where a real
Brucella library at usable depth shares 900 or more. Profiling the reads against GTDB
r220 by containment rather than abundance - the fairer test for a minority organism -
finds Brucella in nine of the eleven at 0.03 to 0.17x coverage with only 4 to 13% of a
reference genome present, which is trace or index-hopping level. ERR775246 reaches 31%
at 0.43x and ERR3218177 reaches 52% at 0.79x, both still far short of a genome.

What the libraries do contain, where we could establish it: ERR471337, ERR554820 and
ERR775236 are Camelimonas fluminis at 98.4% ANI to the type strain; ERR473743 and
ERR775237 are an unnamed Camelimonas species; ERR3218177 is dominated by
Pseudochrobactrum saccharolyticum at 141x. All of those genera sit in Brucellaceae
alongside Brucella, which may explain the original identifications. The five
ERR775244-248 libraries are one organism in the family Alcaligenaceae with no sequenced
representative in proGenomes v4, a dereplicated RefSeq sketch, or GTDB r220.


EVIDENCE AND METHOD

Every per-record verdict, including the records we checked and cleared and the
mislabelled genomes we found in our own reference set in the same pass, is public at
https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under curation/. We are happy to supply
assemblies, allele calls, distance tables or the identification output in whatever form
is most useful, and to route this through the ELIXIR Clearinghouse instead if that is
the preferred mechanism for third-party curation.

If any reading is disputed we would genuinely like to know. The physical isolate
outranks the sequence, and the sequence outranks our reading of it.
