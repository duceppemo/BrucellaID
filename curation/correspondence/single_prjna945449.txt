Subject: SRR25828922 (PRJNA945449) does not appear to contain Brucella

Dear colleagues,

We have been validating a Brucella species-identification tool against every public
Brucella read set in ENA, and one of your records came up - not with a different
Brucella species, but with no detectable Brucella at all.

    run          BioSample       deposited                        what we find
    SRR25828922  SAMN33797231    Brucella pseudogrignonensis      a Bacillus species

  - The reads share 5 of 1000 k-mer hashes with any genome of the Brucella clade. A
    real Brucella library at usable depth shares 900 or more.
  - Profiling the reads against GTDB r220 finds no Brucella at any coverage, and a
    dominant organism at 101x matching Bacillus subtilis at 95.8% average nucleotide
    identity, with 27% of that reference's k-mers present.
  - The library assembles cleanly to 4.08 Mb.

I would stop at genus. 95.8% identity with 27% containment places the organism in
Bacillus but is not enough to name the species, and B. subtilis is simply the nearest
sequenced relative we found rather than a confident match.

What would help most is a check of what was uploaded against what was intended. If
the wrong file went up, the right one may still exist; if the isolate was genuinely
this organism, the record's organism field is the thing to amend. Either way the
record is currently discoverable as Brucella, which is how we came to download it.

If you disagree with the reading I would genuinely like to know.

Every per-record verdict is public at https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under
curation/. Happy to send the assembly or the profiling output.
