Subject: Five Brucella assemblies whose deposited species is contradicted by their
         sequence, and one follow-up to my message of [DATE OF EARLIER EMAIL]

This is a third set of Brucella records. Section 2 is a follow-up that answers a
question I left open last time rather than a new finding.

Methods are as before: MLST-9 (Whatmore et al. scheme) read off the assembly by
exact allele match, Mash distance over a curated 431-genome Brucella reference set
covering all 30 named species of the clade, in silico Bruce-ladder/Suis-ladder/AMOS,
and the real-time assays of Hoover et al. 2026 (doi:10.1128/spectrum.02974-25) run
in silico. Where your own ANI check already reaches a verdict I have quoted it.


1. ONE MORE ST8 ASSEMBLY DEPOSITED AS B. ABORTUS THAT TYPES AS B. MELITENSIS

This is a fifth record of the pattern in section 1 of my earlier message, and your
ANI check already flags it.

    accession        deposited          MLST-9   ANI check              our reading
    GCA_036881765.1  Brucella abortus   ST8      mismatch, melitensis   B. melitensis
                                                 99.91%

    Mash: nearest B. melitensis 0.00074, nearest B. abortus 0.00164. PubMLST records
    ST8 as B. melitensis in 98.3% of 862 isolates. The B. melitensis assay is held in
    full and the B. abortus assay is absent; Bruce-ladder independently calls
    melitensis.


2. THE OTHER HALF OF THE BO1 PAIR (follow-up to section 4 of my earlier message)

Last time I reported GCA_000662015.2, deposited as "Brucella inopinata BO1", and
noted that it sits 0.0237 from GCA_000182725.1, which carries the same strain
designation, so the two records under that strain name are not the same organism. I
said that observation was more useful for triage than my own species call. This is
where the other organism went.

    accession        deposited                      ANI check          our reading
    GCA_000661995.2  Brucella suis bv. 4 str. 40    synonym_match,     B. inopinata
                                                    melitensis 97.83%  (BO1)

    Mash from GCA_000661995.2: nearest B. inopinata 0.00044, and every classical
    species at 0.0224 or more (nearest B. suis 0.0226, nearest B. canis 0.0231). Its
    three nearest neighbours of any species are all deposited B. inopinata.

    The two records are consecutive accessions from one submission, and each one's
    sequence matches the other's deposited name. The PCR panel separates them in the
    same direction: GCA_000662015.2 holds the B. suis assay, while GCA_000661995.2 is
    absent on all three species assays and on the genus marker, which is how the
    deposited B. inopinata controls behave.

    I would treat these two as one correction rather than two. Note that the ANI best
    match for GCA_000661995.2 is B. melitensis at 97.83%, which I do not think is
    informative here: B. inopinata sits around 97-98% from the classical species, so
    the comparison is inside the band where ANI cannot separate them in this genus.


3. ONE ASSEMBLY DEPOSITED AS BRUCELLA SP. THAT IS B. PAPIONIS

This is a naming request rather than a correction, and it is the one I would most
like to see actioned, because the species is currently absent from the archive.

    accession        deposited                      ANI check          our reading
    GCA_000163135.1  Brucella sp. NVSL 07-0026      genus_match,       B. papionis
                                                    microti 99.79%

    Nothing in INSDC is labelled Brucella papionis. A search of the assembly database
    for taxon 1462582 returns nothing, and so does ENA's. This is the only genome of
    the species in the archive, which is why your ANI check can only reach genus: it
    has no B. papionis reference to match against.

    MLST-9 ST54, profile 9-12-20-10-1-3-5-8-2. PubMLST records ST54 as B. papionis in
    both of the isolates that carry it. The same nine alleles, by exact match, are
    carried by an assembly we made from SRR4038994, a read set submitted as
    B. papionis strain B07-0026 (BioSample SAMN05417907); that assembly sits 0.000048
    from GCA_000163135.1, and NVSL 07-0026 is the same strain designation with the
    laboratory prefix on it.

    Mash places it 0.00200 from B. microti, 0.00201 from B. pinnipedialis, 0.00214
    from B. ceti and 0.00239 from B. suis, near-equidistant from four classical
    species and identical to none, which is what a species of its own looks like.


4. ONE ASSEMBLY DEPOSITED AS B. CYTISI THAT IS NOT B. CYTISI

Your ANI check and my reading agree the deposited name is wrong and disagree on the
replacement, so I am only confident of the negative.

    accession        deposited          ANI check             our reading
    GCA_059995225.1  Brucella cytisi    mismatch,             not B. cytisi;
                     (strain WTB2)      lupini 97.75%         nearest B. anthropi

    Mash from GCA_059995225.1: nearest B. anthropi 0.00665, nearest B. lupini 0.01030,
    nearest other B. cytisi 0.03950. All three deposited assemblies of the B. cytisi
    type strain (ESC1, LMG 22713, DSM 19778) agree with each other to 0.0026 and sit
    0.0407, 0.0409 and 0.0425 from this genome, so it is not in that cluster.

    B. anthropi is closer than B. lupini in our data by a factor of 1.5, but both are
    former Ochrobactrum species where within-species diversity is very wide - the
    spread inside deposited B. anthropi reaches 0.21 - so I would not argue for
    anthropi over lupini on 0.0066 against 0.0103. The reliable statement is that the
    genome is not B. cytisi. The PCR panel is silent for this half of the clade and
    offers nothing either way.


5. ONE ASSEMBLY DEPOSITED AS B. CICERI THAT APPEARS TO BE B. INTERMEDIA

I raise this one knowing your ANI check supports the deposited name, so the case
rests on the cluster rather than on a single distance.

    accession        deposited             ANI check            our reading
    GCA_049065255.1  Brucella ciceri       species_match,       B. intermedia
                     (strain SA289)        ciceri 99.14%

    Mash from GCA_049065255.1: 0.00004 to GCA_001917355.1, which is deposited as
    B. intermedia, and 0.00658 to the nearest deposited B. ciceri, including the
    ciceri type strain DSM 22292. It is also 0.000005 from GCA_049065885.1, deposited
    as Brucella sp.

    The strain cluster it belongs to (SA148, SA289, IR068 and their neighbours) is
    deposited as B. intermedia in 12 of 14 records and as B. ciceri in 2. Every member
    of that cluster is further from the ciceri type strain than the within-species
    spread of deposited B. ciceri, so if the cluster is ciceri then the type strain is
    the outlier, which seems the less likely reading.

    I appreciate 99.14% clears the species threshold. My difficulty is the one I
    raised last time: in this genus almost any pair of species clears it, so a
    species_match does not establish that the deposited name is the closest available
    one. Here the deposited name is 0.0066 away and another deposited name is 0.00004
    away.


WHAT I AM NOT ASKING ABOUT

Nine further assemblies are flagged in our curation but rest on distance alone, in
parts of the clade where the MLST and both PCR assays are silent and where
within-species diversity is wide enough that "nearer to another species" is
suggestive and no more. I am not proposing changes to those and have not listed them
here; I can send them if a list of records worth a second look is useful to you.

Two assemblies deposited as B. abortus biovar 1 (the BCB027 and BCB013 genomes) are
clearly not ordinary B. abortus by every method we applied, but the methods disagree
on what they are, so I have no name to propose.
