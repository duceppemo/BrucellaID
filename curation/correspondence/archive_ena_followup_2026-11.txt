Subject: Follow-up to our third-party curation request: one further Brucella
         BioSample, resolved by ANI to the type strain

To the ENA curation team,

This adds one record to the request we sent on 2026-11-07. It was within that message's
scope but deliberately left out, because at the time it rested on Mash distance alone in
a part of the clade where that is not enough to name a species. We have since resolved
it properly, by average nucleotide identity to the type strain of each candidate
species, and it is mislabelled.

  BioSample          run           deposited       proposed
  SAMEA114240991     ERR11807265   B. anthropi     B. intermedia

  Under 88% ANI to the B. anthropi type strain ATCC 49188, so anthropi is wrong. It is
  99.61% to the B. ciceri type strain DSM 22292 and 98.31% to the B. intermedia type
  strain CIP 105838. Those two type strains are 98.26% ANI to each other and so one
  species by the usual criterion; B. intermedia (Velasco et al. 1998) is the senior name
  over B. ciceri (Imran et al. 2010), which is why we propose intermedia.

The library was assembled with SKESA for this purpose, to 4.95 Mb, which is the size
range of the former Ochrobactrum species. The record is in study PRJEB59403.

ONE THING WORTH RECORDING ALONGSIDE THESE

The same comparison found that four pairs of named species in this group have type
strains at or above 95% ANI, and so are not separable by the standard species criterion:

    B. intermedia / B. ciceri        98.26%
    B. anthropi   / B. lupini        97.14%
    B. lupini     / B. cytisi        95.95%
    B. anthropi   / B. cytisi        95.51%

We mention it because it bears on how any request of this kind should be read. Within
those groups a species name is partly a nomenclatural choice rather than a measurement,
and we have applied the senior name where we propose one. It is also why we are not
proposing changes to several other records in this part of the clade where the deposited
name is the closest type-strain match, even when it clears 95% against two others.

Every per-record verdict, the type-strain panel and the full ANI matrix are public at
https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under curation/.
