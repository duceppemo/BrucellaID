Subject: SRR31357708 (PRJNA1183633) types as Brucella anthropi rather than B. cytisi

Dear colleagues,

We have been validating a Brucella species-identification tool against every public
Brucella read set in ENA, and one of your records came up.

    run          BioSample       deposited         the sequence says
    SRR31357708  SAMN44729562    Brucella cytisi   Brucella anthropi

This one rests on distance rather than on the usual two independent methods, so I
want to be clear about what it does and does not establish. The former Ochrobactrum
species do not carry the classical Brucella MLST alleles - six of the nine loci got no
read at all - and neither the Bruce-ladder nor the real-time assays we use cover that
half of the clade. So there is no second method available here.

What the distances show, over a curated 431-genome reference set covering all 30 named
species:

  - 0.0076 from the nearest B. anthropi genome, and every one of the eight nearest
    matches is B. anthropi.
  - 0.0381, 0.0383 and 0.0394 from the three deposited assemblies of the B. cytisi
    type strain (ESC1, LMG 22713, DSM 19778). Those three agree with each other to
    0.0026, so they form a tight cluster that this read set is not in. They rank 89th,
    90th and 91st of the 431 references.
  - The library assembles to 4.84 Mb, which is the size range of the former
    Ochrobactrum species rather than the 3.3 Mb of a classical Brucella genome, so it
    is independently in the right part of the clade.

The honest summary is that the B. cytisi label is hard to reconcile with the sequence,
and B. anthropi is the best available reading. Within that group the within-species
spread is very wide - inside deposited B. anthropi it reaches 0.21 - so I would not
argue hard for anthropi over another neighbour on 0.0076 alone.

If you hold the isolate, that would settle it better than we can. If you disagree I
would genuinely like to know: the physical isolate outranks the sequence, and the
sequence outranks our reading of it.

Every per-record verdict is public at https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under
curation/, including the errors we found in our own reference set in the same pass.
