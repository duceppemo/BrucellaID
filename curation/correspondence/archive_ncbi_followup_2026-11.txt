Subject: Follow-up to our third-party curation request: two further Brucella
         BioSamples, resolved by ANI to the type strain

To the NCBI BioSample team,

This adds two records to the request we sent on 2026-11-07. They were in that message's
scope but were deliberately left out of it, because at the time they rested on Mash
distance alone in a part of the clade where that is not enough to name a species. We
have since resolved them properly, by average nucleotide identity to the type strain of
each candidate species, and both are mislabelled.

  BioSample       run           deposited                proposed
  SAMN07581394    SRR6003475    B. thiophenivorans       B. pituitosa
  SAMN13812480    SRR10948139   B. anthropi              B. intermedia

  SAMN07581394   98.51% ANI to the B. pituitosa type strain CCUG 50899. The
                 B. thiophenivorans type strain DSM 7216 falls below the 85% floor our
                 comparison reports, so the deposited species is not a near miss.

  SAMN13812480   Under 88% ANI to the B. anthropi type strain ATCC 49188, so anthropi is
                 wrong. It is 98.67% to the B. ciceri type strain DSM 22292 and 97.81%
                 to the B. intermedia type strain CIP 105838. Those two type strains are
                 98.26% ANI to each other and so one species by the usual criterion;
                 B. intermedia (Velasco et al. 1998) is the senior name over B. ciceri
                 (Imran et al. 2010), which is why we propose intermedia.

Both libraries were assembled with SKESA for this purpose, to 4.63 and 4.77 Mb, which is
the size range of the former Ochrobactrum species.

ONE THING WORTH RECORDING ALONGSIDE THESE

The same comparison found that four pairs of named species in this group have type
strains at or above 95% ANI, and so are not separable by the standard species criterion:

    B. intermedia / B. ciceri        98.26%
    B. anthropi   / B. lupini        97.14%
    B. lupini     / B. cytisi        95.95%
    B. anthropi   / B. cytisi        95.51%

We mention it because it bears on how any request of this kind should be read. Within
those groups a species name is partly a nomenclatural choice rather than a measurement,
and we have applied the senior name where we propose one. It is also why we are not
proposing changes to several other records in this part of the clade: SRR1798201
(SAMN02744640, B. lupini) is 98.59% to its own type strain and we consider it correct,
even though it also clears 95% against anthropi and cytisi.

Every per-record verdict, the type-strain panel and the full ANI matrix are public at
https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under curation/.
