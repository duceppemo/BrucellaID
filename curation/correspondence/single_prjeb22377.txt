Subject: ERR3218177 (PRJEB22377) is dominated by Pseudochrobactrum, with Brucella a
         minority

Dear colleagues,

We have been validating a Brucella species-identification tool against every public
Brucella read set in ENA. One record in PRJEB22377 came up, and separately thirteen
records in PRJEB4782, which I am writing about under separate cover.

    run          BioSample          deposited          what the library holds
    ERR3218177   SAMEA104338291     Brucella abortus   mostly Pseudochrobactrum
                                                       saccharolyticum, with a
                                                       minority Brucella

Profiling the reads against GTDB r220:

  - Pseudochrobactrum saccharolyticum at 98.8% average nucleotide identity, 141x
    coverage, with 70% of that reference's k-mers present. This is the bulk of the
    library.
  - Brucella at 0.79x coverage with 52% of the reference genome present. Real but a
    small minority, which is why our sketch-based identification reported the sample
    as not identified: the read sketch is dominated by the other organism.

  The library assembles to 3.95 Mb and shares 6 of 1000 k-mer hashes with the clade.

Pseudochrobactrum may be the more interesting part. It sits in Brucellaceae, the same
family as Brucella, and is a slow-growing gram-negative that has been reported from
clinical and veterinary material, so an isolate typed biochemically could reasonably
have been recorded as Brucella. We saw the same pattern in PRJEB4782, where five
libraries deposited as B. abortus are Camelimonas, another Brucellaceae genus.

Given the minority Brucella is genuinely present, this looks more like a mixed culture
or a mixed library than a straightforward mislabel, so a note on the record may serve
a reader better than a relabel - and if the isolate is still held, it may be worth
re-examining for purity.

To correct the record: ENA has no third-party route for this, so it goes through the
support form at https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/support, or through the ELIXIR
Clearinghouse, which exists for third-party curation of records one did not submit. We
are glad to raise it there with the evidence attached if that is easier.

If you disagree with the reading I would genuinely like to know.

Every per-record verdict is public at https://github.com/duceppemo/BrucellaID under
curation/, including the errors we found in our own reference set in the same pass.
